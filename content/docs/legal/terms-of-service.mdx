## 6. Bounties

A bounty, posted by a requester, describes a place to capture and the reward on offer. Before any work starts, the reward is placed in USDC escrow, which lets a contributor verify the reward exists before investing time in a capture. That escrow remains locked until either the requester accepts a submission or the bounty is cancelled or expires.

- **Acceptance.** Accepting a submission automatically releases the escrow to the contributor and sends the capture on to reconstruction. Once given, acceptance cannot be reversed.
- **Cancellation and expiry.** Up until a submission is accepted, a requester is free to cancel the bounty. Should the bounty expire without any accepted submission, the escrowed reward goes back to the requester by the same route it was funded.
- **Requester obligations.** Submissions must be evaluated in good faith; the review process must not be exploited to extract usable data without accepting and paying for it.
- **Contributor obligations.** Only captures that genuinely meet the brief may be submitted.
