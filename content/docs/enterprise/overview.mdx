Two things come with Utopia for enterprise: ground-level spatial data whose provenance can be verified, and the ability to commission captures of specific locations through bounties.

The intended users are teams that train and operate physical systems: robotics, autonomous platforms, simulation and mapping.

## What you get

3D reconstructions of real places. Each model traces back to its source capture, and each capture carries a cryptographic fingerprint made at the moment of recording. Delivery format is a textured mesh in GLB.

## Where to start

Either open [app.utopiadata.net](https://app.utopiadata.net) and pick **Buy data**, or navigate directly to [data.utopiadata.net](https://data.utopiadata.net).

## Commissioned capture

When coverage does not include the place you need, a bounty gets it captured. Describe the location and what matters about it, put up a reward, then review incoming submissions and accept the one that fits. Funding is the reward plus a 10% platform fee, and that fee already includes reconstruction.

## Data residency

Regional routing of data is supported. The available regions are us-east-1, eu-west-1 and apac-1. Cross-region access only works when explicitly configured on the account.

## Access

Access is early stage and issued per pilot.
