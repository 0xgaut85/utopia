The enterprise agreement for a deployment is where its service level terms are defined, scoped to whatever regions and workloads that deployment includes.

<Callout type="info">
  Send your availability and support requirements to [hello@utopiadata.net](mailto:hello@utopiadata.net).
</Callout>

## What a service level includes

- Uptime of the spatial query, ingestion and streaming endpoints
- Uptime of authentication and key management
- Committed support response times

## What it excludes

Three things fall outside service level terms: maintenance windows that were announced ahead of time, force majeure events, and how densely any particular geographic area is covered. Coverage depends on contributor activity in a place, not on infrastructure being up. If somewhere you need is uncovered, a [bounty](/docs/contribute/bounties) is the way to commission it.
