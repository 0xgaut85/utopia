---
title: Quickstart
description: From wallet connection to your first paid capture. Record, submit to a bounty and receive the reward.
---

Everything begins at [app.utopiadata.net](https://app.utopiadata.net), where you choose between two paths: **Capture data** if you are contributing, or **Buy data** if your organization is commissioning captures.

Choosing **Capture data** launches the capture app at [app.utopiadata.net/capture](https://app.utopiadata.net/capture). Any browser will run it and there is nothing to install. Android users can alternatively grab it from Google Play; both versions are the same app.

Choosing **Buy data** takes you to [data.utopiadata.net](https://data.utopiadata.net), the marketplace where bounties get posted and fulfilled.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    The wallet is the whole sign-in. No email address, no password, nothing else to keep track of.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up a little gas if you intend to upload">
    Gas is required for uploading captures and nothing else. Looking through open bounties costs nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a bounty">
    On [data.utopiadata.net](https://data.utopiadata.net), check what each bounty is asking for, the location it covers and its remaining time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record and submit">
    Capture the location following the [capture guidance](/docs/contribute/capture) and get your clip in before the deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect the reward">
    Once the requester accepts your submission, the payout from Utopia happens automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Just browsing for now">
  Every open bounty on [data.utopiadata.net](https://data.utopiadata.net) is visible without a wallet connected. The wallet only becomes necessary at the point of uploading.
</Callout>
