As an enterprise platform, Utopia prices each deployment individually. The quote reflects your query volume, your streaming throughput and any data sovereignty requirements you bring.

<Callout type="info">
  Send a short note about your use case to [hello@utopiadata.net](mailto:hello@utopiadata.net) and we will come back with a proposal built around it.
</Callout>

## What goes into the price

- **Spatial query volume.** How many /v1/spatial/query calls you make per month.
- **Streaming throughput.** How many concurrent streams you run and the event volume they carry.
- **Data ingestion.** The amount of video you upload and its processing.
- **Coverage regions.** Which geographic areas your account is authorized for.
- **Service level.** Defined in the enterprise contract.
- **Sovereign deployments.** Dedicated infrastructure for defense and government customers.
