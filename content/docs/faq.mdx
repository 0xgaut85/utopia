---
title: FAQ
description: Answers on wallet sign-in, invite codes, reconstruction times, refunds, payouts and points.
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What do I sign in with?">
    Your wallet is the entire login. No email or password exists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is gas required to take part?">
    Only when uploading captures. You can browse open bounties without any.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does submitting a capture cost me anything?">
    No. The bounty reward covers reconstruction, so the contributor never pays for it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long until my capture is reconstructed?">
    Expect 30 minutes at minimum; longer or more complex captures take more time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the requester ever see my original video?">
    No. What they get is the completed 3D model. Your raw footage never leaves your side.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is it possible to cancel a bounty after posting it?">
    Yes, right up until you accept a submission. Should the deadline expire with no accepted submission, the reward comes back to you through the same channel you funded it with.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="At what point does a contributor get paid?">
    Utopia releases the reward the instant your submission is accepted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do points do?">
    Points track how much you have contributed to the network and where you rank in it. They exist independently of bounty rewards.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
